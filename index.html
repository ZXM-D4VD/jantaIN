Frequently Asked Questions

How are MCD requests handled?
Requests are logged into our database and forwarded to local ward officers for immediate field resolution.
Will I get an email confirmation?
Yes, as soon as you submit the form, EmailJS triggers a unique ticket ID to your registered email address.
How can I track my request status?
You can use your Ticket ID on the Admin Dashboard or contact local helpline representatives.
